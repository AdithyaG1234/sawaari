Sawaari privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. This covers the Sawaari apps for Android and iPhone.

The short version

Sawaari has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your rides, garage, SOS contacts and medical notes stay on your phone. Only what a group ride needs is shared, and only with the people in your ride group.

What is shared with your ride group

When you create or join a ride group, these go to the group through Google Firebase, under an anonymous ID made for your phone:

  • the name you type in, and your role in the group,
  • your live location and speed while you are in the group, including with the screen off,
  • chat messages and voice notes you send,
  • the ride plan and meeting point the leader shares, and SOS alerts you send,
  • on Android only, if you choose to: your phone number and an emergency contact's number.

Anyone with the group's 6-letter code can join and see this. Leaving the group stops sharing.

What stays on your phone

Recorded rides, your vehicles and service history, emergency contacts, medical notes, settings and downloaded maps. SOS text messages are sent from your own phone to your own contacts.

Other services the app uses

  • Maps: map tiles are downloaded from OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap servers.
  • Search and routes (iPhone): what you search for, and the points of a route you plan, are sent to Photon and the FOSSGIS Valhalla server to get an answer. Sawaari does not store them.
  • Place ratings (Android): a rating you give a place is shared with other Sawaari riders under your anonymous ID.

Permissions

Location shows you on the map, guides you and shares your position with your group. The microphone records voice notes only while you hold the mic button. Motion sensors are used for crash detection, on the phone only. Notifications are for group alerts, crash countdowns and service reminders.

Children

Sawaari is meant for riders and is not aimed at children.

Contact

Questions or a request to delete group data: open an issue at github.com/AdithyaG1234/sawaari/issues.
